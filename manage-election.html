Voting Rules

Students must be in this election's eligible voter list to cast a vote.

Students who are registered in the general NUNSA-FUD student database but are not on this election's voter list can still enter the website and follow the election, but cannot vote.

Voting will automatically become available at the scheduled start time and automatically end at the scheduled end time.

Elections are not manually paused.
