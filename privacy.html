Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026

What we keep about you

  • Your registration number, name and academic level, from the official student list.
  • Your email address, which you give when you create your account. We use it to send verification and recovery codes.
  • Your PIN, stored as a one-way scramble. Nobody, including the admins, can read it.
  • Your profile picture, if you add one.
  • Applications and mandates you submit, and announcements you post if you hold an office.
  • Your Telegram chat, only if you link the NUNSA-FUD bot.

How voting is recorded

Voting creates two separate records. One says that you voted, when, and your receipt code. The other holds the choices on the ballot, with no name, registration number or link back to you. Admins can see who voted. They cannot see how anyone voted.

Who can see what

  • You see your own details and receipts.
  • Signed-in students see candidates' names, levels, pictures and mandates, and published results.
  • Election admins see student records and voter lists so they can run the elections.

Cookies and tracking

There are no ads and no tracking cookies. The home page counts visits as anonymous daily totals with no names, IP addresses or device details. Your IP address is used only to slow down abuse, such as repeated wrong PINs, and is kept in a scrambled form.

Corrections and removal

If your details are wrong, or you want them removed, ask the NUNSA-FUD election admins. Some election records have to be kept so results can be checked.
